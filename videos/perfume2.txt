A raw flash-lit luxury fragrance still life on rumpled white cotton bedding, viewed from a slightly angled top-down perspective. A rectangular glass perfume bottle lies diagonally across the frame, its transparent edges catching sharp white flash highlights, with warm amber liquid visible through the glass. Beside it, a few scattered dried rose petals, a single gold earring and a small pool of spilled golden-brown perfume soaking into the fabric.

The composition feels spontaneous and unposed, like the morning after an elegant party. The bedding is soft, creased and uneven, with deep folds creating shadow pockets across the white fabric. The top of the frame falls into deep darkness.

Lighting is direct on-camera flash: hard, frontal, slightly harsh, with crisp shadows under the bottle, blown highlights on the glass and visible texture across the cotton weave. Raw, documentary and editorial rather than polished studio product photography.

Material realism is essential: transparent glass refraction, glossy amber liquid staining the fabric, dried petals, soft cotton wrinkles, subtle dust, fingerprint smudges on the glass and faint analog grain.

Mood: moody, glamorous, effortless, high-fashion fragrance campaign captured with the honesty of a disposable camera flash.
